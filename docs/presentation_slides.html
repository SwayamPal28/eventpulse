Existing Approaches / Literature Review

"Go, change the world"
Research Paper Title & Authors Relevance to Our Project (EventPulse)
"BASE: An Acid Alternative"
by Dan Pritchett (ACM Queue) 		Justifies dropping slow SQL Two-Phase Commits (2PC) in favor of eventual consistency, Message Queues, and the Transactional Outbox pattern used in our checkout flow.
"Design and Implementation of High Concurrency Ticketing System Based on Redis and RabbitMQ"
by Y. Zhang, et al. (IEEE) 		Directly validates our choice of using Redis Lua scripts for atomic sub-millisecond seat holds and RabbitMQ for asynchronous ticket processing.
"Microservices: Yesterday, Today, and Tomorrow"
by Nicola Dragoni, et al. 		Provides theoretical foundation for our Bounded Contexts, mathematically justifying why isolating the read-heavy seatmap from the write-heavy reservation engine prevents system crashes.
"Performance Evaluation of Microservices in Docker and Kubernetes"
by D. N. Jha, et al. 		Supports our container orchestration strategy, detailing how Kubernetes Horizontal Pod Auto-scaling and health probes handle sudden flash-sale traffic spikes.
"Infrastructure as Code: A Systematic Literature Review"
by J. C. e Silva, et al. 		Backs our DevOps methodology of using Terraform and LocalStack to eliminate manual configuration drift and deploy fully reproducible cloud-native environments.
